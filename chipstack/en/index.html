Inside the app

Everything your group needs.

Real views from the app, with a made-up example group.

01 · Rotation

Everyone gets a turn.

After every night, the next person automatically moves up. If someone can’t make it on short notice, a substitute host steps in – with undo.

The person who created the group sets the order.

02 · Dates

One date for everyone.

The whole group sees the date, time and place of the next night. Any member can change it.

Past nights are listed below.

03 · Stats

Who hosted how often.

History and stats show how many nights you have had and who hosted most often.

04 · Invite

Only those invited are in.

Invite by link, QR code or code. Each person only sees the groups they were invited to.

The person who created the group can recover access with a recovery code.

Good to know

Only what the group needs.

  • No account

    No email address, no phone number, no password. The invite is your way in.

  • No tracking

    No ads, no analytics tools. Chip Stack stores the group name, your names, dates and the rotation order. More in the privacy policy.

  • No gambling

    No real money, no betting, no payouts and no winnings. The app only organizes the night.
