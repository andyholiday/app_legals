2. Summary

  • ShipTrip requires no user account and uses no developer-owned servers. I do not receive your travel data.
  • Cruises, photos, and your journal stay on your device. There is no ShipTrip cloud sync.
  • ShipTrip contains no ads and does not itself integrate analytics, tracking, or crash reporting services. Google's maps SDK does collect its own usage and crash data (section 5).
  • Data leaves the device only in these ways: Google Maps, AI import through Google Gemini, Android backup and device transfer, your calendar account, export and sharing. See sections 5 to 10.
  • The purchase runs through Google Play (section 11).

3. Data processed in ShipTrip

Depending on your use, ShipTrip processes in particular:

  • cruise title, travel dates, cruise line, and ship
  • cabin and booking details
  • routes, ports, port times, sea days, and excursions
  • notes, ratings, expenses, and journal entries (including mood)
  • cruise, port, and journal photos you select
  • wish-list trips and your own cruise lines and ships
  • app settings, calendar selection, and links to calendar events created by ShipTrip
  • an optional Gemini API key
  • a random installation identifier the app uses to detect a device change

4. Storage on your device

ShipTrip stores app data in the app's private storage on your device. ShipTrip does not add its own encryption to travel data and photos; Android's device encryption applies.

The Gemini API key is stored encrypted on the device. The Android Keystore manages the key used for that encryption. The API key is excluded from Android backup and from device transfer.

5. Map with Google Maps

The map in ShipTrip uses Google's Maps SDK for Android. When you open and use the map, the SDK loads map material directly from Google. The app has no switch for this. The map is a fixed part of ShipTrip.

According to Google, the SDK collects:

  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived
  • request and device metadata
  • crash data and stack traces from the SDK
  • a unique Maps SDK identifier
  • map interactions such as panning and zooming

Google also receives the map area requested. It may reveal the region of your trips. ShipTrip does not ask for your device's location.

According to Google, it uses this data to provide and improve its services. The data goes directly to Google, not to a developer-owned server. The Google Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms apply between Google and me.

More information: Google's Maps SDK data disclosure, Google Privacy Policy, Google Maps End User Additional Terms of Service. Google may transfer data to the USA and states that it is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

6. Optional AI import with Google Gemini

AI import is not configured by default. It becomes available only after you confirm the notice about Google Gemini and store your own Gemini API key. You are Google's contracting party for this.

  • When validating the key, ShipTrip sends a short test prompt and the key directly to the Gemini API.
  • Each time you start an AI import, ShipTrip sends the complete text you pasted into the import field, extraction instructions, and the key directly to Google over HTTPS. Photos and other files are not sent.
  • I do not receive your API key, booking text, or Google's response on any developer-owned server.

Google states that prompts, context, and outputs are retained for 55 days for abuse monitoring. Authorized Google employees may review flagged content for this purpose. For unpaid services, Google may also use inputs and outputs to improve its products, and human reviewers may read content. For users in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, and the United Kingdom, Google states that the Paid Services data-use terms apply even to unpaid quota. For Paid Services, Google states that prompts and responses are not used for product improvement.

Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or unnecessary personal information. A booking confirmation may, for example, contain your name, address, or email address.

Use is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms, the abuse monitoring documentation, and the Google Privacy Policy. Google requires API users to be at least 18 years old and requires a Paid Service for API clients made available in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom. Use the Gemini feature only if you meet these requirements. For transfers to the USA, see Google's statement in section 5.

7. Android backup and device transfer

Android Auto Backup (backup in your Google Account)

If backup is turned on for your device, Android saves ShipTrip's settings in a private area of your Google Account. These include:

  • intro status, color scheme, and reminder settings
  • calendar sync settings, the identifier of the selected calendar, and internal notes on events still to be removed
  • your confirmation of the Gemini notice
  • the random value the app uses to detect a device change

Cruises, photos, your journal, and the Gemini API key are not part of this backup. You turn backup on or off in Android settings. I have no access to this backup. According to Google, it is end-to-end encrypted using your device's PIN, pattern, or password. More information: Google's description of Auto Backup.

Device transfer

If you move to a new device using Android's transfer feature, ShipTrip's database and photos also go directly to the new device by cable or Wi-Fi. The Gemini API key does not move; you enter it again on the new device.

8. Optional calendar sync and reminders

If you turn on calendar sync, ShipTrip asks for read and write access to your calendar. The app lists writable calendars and creates, updates, or removes its own events in them, with cruise title, dates, port and place details, cruise line, and ship. It reads only the calendar list and its own ShipTrip events.

Depending on the calendar you choose, your account provider, such as Google or Exchange, syncs these events to its cloud. This happens outside the app. That provider's terms apply.

ShipTrip schedules cruise reminders as local Android notifications on the device. For this, the app asks for the notification permission. After a device restart, it schedules the reminders again. They show a cruise's title and date. ShipTrip uses no push server for this.

9. Photos

ShipTrip receives only the photos you deliberately select in the Android photo picker. The app does not ask for permission to access your photo gallery. It copies the selected photos into its private storage.

The copies keep embedded metadata. Originals may therefore contain, for example, the capture location (GPS) or capture time. A full ZIP backup keeps image data unchanged. When you share a single cruise, images are instead re-encoded as JPEG without metadata.

10. Export, import, and sharing

You can save your data, including photos and journal entries, as a ZIP archive to a location you choose in Android's file dialog. You can import ZIP and supported JSON files.

You can give a single cruise as a .shiptrip file to an app or person of your choice through the Android share menu. The file may also contain the route, expenses, and photos. From that point on, you and the recipient decide on further processing. You can import cruise files you receive.

For sharing, ShipTrip places the file in a temporary export folder of the app. This folder stays for at least 24 hours because Android does not report when the recipient has read the file. After that, ShipTrip deletes it the next time the app starts or the next time you share. You must delete copies outside the app yourself.

They contain, for example, order number, date, product, amount, and country, and depending on the case also region, city, postal code, and device model. 12. Retention and deletion

App data stays on your device until you delete it. You can do this in the following ways:

  • Remove API Key: deletes the Gemini API key and the related key in the Android Keystore.
  • Delete All Data: deletes cruises, wish-list trips, your own cruise lines and ships, hidden entries, their photos, and scheduled reminders. You can choose to delete or keep the API key.
  • Reset App: deletes all travel data and photos, temporary storage including the export folder, scheduled reminders, the API key, and settings. The app removes its calendar events if calendar access is still allowed. Only the random installation identifier, internal calendar notes, and your confirmation of the Gemini notice remain on the device until you uninstall the app.
  • Turning off calendar sync: removes the events ShipTrip created if calendar access is still allowed.
  • Uninstalling: Android removes all data in the app's private storage, including the API key.

You must delete the following outside the app yourself: exported files, shared copies, and calendar events you did not remove through calendar sync before uninstalling.

Android manages the backup of settings in your Google Account. According to Google, only the most recent backup is kept, and obsolete backups are deleted automatically after a period of inactivity. Google's stated periods and rules apply to Gemini and Google Maps.
